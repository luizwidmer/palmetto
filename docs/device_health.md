# Device-loss attribution: bounded readiness instrument

**Status:** the helper, device-lost hooks, and pure record/parse test are implemented; integration and the Palmetto build gate are still pending. No build/relink or boot arm was run for this slice. A read-only readiness sample at `2026-11-27T09:30:48.5840841-03:00` found no `palmetto` process and no `D:\leonida-work\.boot-lock`. The lead deferred the next arm until integration clears.

This page correlates existing loss observations with their arm settings. It does **not** assign a cause. Times in the tables are elapsed seconds from each arm's start unless marked otherwise; no host-local time-of-day is converted.

## Switch and record

`PALMETTO_DEVICE_HEALTH=1` enables one bounded record per D3D12/Vulkan device. Unset, empty, `0`, `true`, or other spellings leave it off. A loss line has the form:

```text
[rt] d3d12: device-health v1 lost_after_ms=... last_submit_ms=... submit_age_ms=... last_workload=... attempted=... arena_live_bytes=... arena_peak_bytes=... arena_capacity_bytes=... arena_allocations=... image_count=... image_bytes=... graphics_pipelines_live=... graphics_pipelines_created=... graphics_pipeline_cap=... deferred_built=...
```

- `lost_after_ms`, `last_submit_ms`, and `submit_age_ms` use a monotonic clock from `DeviceState` construction. `last_workload` is the last command-list category whose existing submit callback reported success. Categories are `draw`, `dispatch`, `indirect`, `ray-build`, `copy`, `mixed`, `other`, and `empty`.
- `attempted` is the prepared batch associated with a failed submit when loss is noticed there. It is **not** evidence that the driver accepted or executed that batch. With no prior successful submit, `last_workload` is `unknown`; without a matching attempted batch, `attempted` is `unknown`. Unavailable numeric counters print `n/a`.
- Arena fields are the cached allocator counters. Graphics pipeline fields are the cached live-budget count/cap and cumulative graphics PSO count; `deferred_built` is the existing deferred-compute counter. Image count/bytes are read only when the separate `PALMETTO_IMAGE_MEM_TRACE=1` census is already enabled and its lock is immediately available; otherwise they are `n/a`.
- The output buffer is 640 bytes including its NUL terminator and is emitted once. Snapshot locks use `try_lock`; a busy counter is `n/a`, not a wait. There is no resource walk, Vulkan/driver query, memory-budget query, dump of resource addresses, or `vkGetDeviceFaultInfoEXT` call. The switch does not change submission, creation, or recovery behavior.

The seam and GPU-free checks live in [device_health.h](../port/src/device_health.h) and [device_health_test.cpp](../port/test/device_health_test.cpp). The standalone test compiled with MSVC outside `port/build` and passed 54 checks. Per the integration rule, [port/CMakeLists.txt](../port/CMakeLists.txt) was not changed; the lead will register the CMake/CTest target after coordination.

## Peer-reported mechanisms (external evidence only)

The checked Five Star Recomp docs at revision `34b4dfed` describe an NVIDIA **D3D12** internal shader-table ceiling of 512 MiB, background shader recompiles that can fill it, and a driver-background-work disable request that Windows may refuse when Developer Mode is off; their guard uses a driver-read table or refused-disable result rather than treating an estimate as proof. Separately, its optional keep-awake compute loop lost an RTX 5090 at 100–154 s in every reported on boot, while a no-loop control ran 660 s. References: `docs/running.md` (“Developer mode”) and `docs/loader.md` (`--driver-background-work`, `--no-shader-table-guard`, `--gpu-keep-awake`). These are not Vulkan measurements from this host; this project does not run that keep-awake loop or read the NVIDIA shader tables.

The Victory-fork README at revision `9a9091d1` reports device removals after 30–50 minutes, says memory use (84–92% of budget) was similar in runs with and without loss, and leaves the cause open. It also discusses a 64 KiB constant-view cap as a hypothesis from another author's card, not a demonstrated cause on its test machine. Its loss instrumentation records recent context and coarse memory/pipeline information. Reference: `README.md`, entries dated 2026-11-26. This is external evidence, not proof of a matching mechanism here. No peer code or mechanism was transplanted.

## Limits and next falsifier

- The device-health line exists only when this layer sees a loss through a failed submit, present result, or `GetDeviceRemovedReason`. A driver event with no layer-observed loss (as in `s61`/`s62`) produces no line.
- `last_workload` identifies a command category, not a shader, resource, GPU queue timeline, or root cause. `attempted` may never have reached the driver. The budget is a cached software snapshot, not VRAM usage, a vendor shader-table reading, DRED, or a page-fault address.
- The prior fault-report experiment (`df2`) crashed in the vendor driver. Do not call `vkGetDeviceFaultInfoEXT`; the helper intentionally avoids it.
- Existing arms mix title-side faults, timeouts, host exceptions, and device losses. A stopped title is not automatically a device loss, and correlation with `PALMETTO_TITLE_CLASS0A_SIZE`, submission overlap, frame pacing, or pipeline hold is not causation.

**Next falsifier (after the lead clears integration and the test/docs gate):** at most one light helper-enabled retry of the existing `PALMETTO_SUBMIT_OVERLAP=1` arm, using the same light/content template and all other switches held fixed, plus `PALMETTO_DEVICE_HEALTH=1`. Compare its `lost_after_ms`, last-successful/attempted workload, submit age, and coarse arena/graphics-pipeline snapshot with the historical `overlap=1` losses at 64.3/68.5 s and the `overlap=0` arm that survived 130.3 s. Keep validation, frame pacing and pipeline hold as in that baseline; do not add a keep-awake workload or use a fault-report API. Record explicit start/end offsets and stop after at most two device losses total. A repeated loss with similar timing/workload and snapshot would improve repeatability but still not prove cause; no recurrence or a materially different context weakens the overlap association. A matched helper-enabled off/on pair or a later one-factor probe of another peer-reported mechanism needs a separate lead decision. No arm is part of this slice.
